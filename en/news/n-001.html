New Trends in Smart Lighting: Matter Protocol Brings New Possibilities

Matter protocol is reshaping the smart lighting ecosystem. Matter Over WiFi and Matter Over Thread enable smart lighting products to seamlessly integrate with Apple HomeKit, Amazon Alexa, and Google Home.

For manufacturers, Matter reduces R&D costs for multi-platform adaptation; for consumers, cross-brand and cross-platform device interactions become simpler.
