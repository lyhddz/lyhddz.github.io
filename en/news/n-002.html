How to Choose the Right WiFi LED Controller: A Buying Guide

WiFi LED controller is the core component of smart lighting systems. Consider the following dimensions:

1. Protocol compatibility: Besides WiFi, does it support 2.4G, Zigbee?

2. Channels: Single color, CCT, or RGBW?

3. App experience: Compatible with major smart home platforms?

4. Load capacity: Maximum wattage?
