HR1 30A Knob Dimming Controller(2.4G)

• 2.4G RF wireless technology • Switchable DIP switch for adjusting PWM frequency and dimming curve • Remote control distance: 30m

Features

  • The single-color 30A knob dimming controller adopts the widely used 2.4GHz wireless technology, featuring low power consumption, long signal transmission distance, and strong anti-interference capability. It supports wireless remote control for convenient operation.
  • 2.4G RF wireless technology
  • Switchable DIP switch for adjusting PWM frequency and dimming curve
  • Remote control distance: 30m
  • Auto-forwarding: Automatically forward the remote control signal to another controller, making the remote control distance infinite
  • Have DND function, usually used in power failure area and save power
  • Support TUYA SMART app control (2.4GHz gateway is needed)
  • Support third party voice control like Alexa, Google assistant,Yandex Alice, Tmall Genie,Baidu and Xiaomi (2.4GHz gateway is needed)
  • Parameters
  • Product Name: 30A Knob Dimming Controller(2.4G)
  • Model No.: HR1
  • Input Voltage: DC12-24V
  • Output Voltage: DC12~24V
  • Output Current: Max 30A
  • Control Distance: 30M
  • RF： 2.4GHz
  • Working Temperature: -10~40℃
  • EMC Standard (EMC): ETSI EN301 489-1 v 2.2.3 (2019-11) ETSI EN 301 489-3 v 2.3.2 (2023-01)
  • Safety Standard (LVD): EN 61347-1：2015+A1: 2021 EN 61347-2-11: 2001+A1:2019
  • Radio Equipment (RED): ETSI EN 300 440 V2.2.1
  • Certification: CE, EMC, RED
