Company Introduction

Founded in 2008, FUTLIGHT is a high-tech enterprise specializing in R&D, manufacturing and sales of intelligent electronic products. Headquartered in Shenzhen, China, with over 8,000 square meters of production area, 8 modern production lines and more than 100 employees.

To become a globally leading supplier of intelligent electronic products, driving industry advancement through innovation.

To provide customers with excellent intelligent electronic products and services, creating a smarter and more convenient lifestyle.
