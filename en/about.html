About Us

Company Introduction

Luoyang Heding Electromechanical Equipment Co., Ltd. is located in Luoyang, one of China's ancient capitals. In recent years the company has grown steadily and now comprises one sales company, two sales divisions and one high-tech R&D centre, forming an integrated technology, industry and trade enterprise with strong technical strength and a broad service network. Our customers are spread across industries worldwide. The company holds 3 invention patents; our self-developed products carry independent intellectual property rights under the registered trademark Hdintool. We employ professional technicians and operate advanced processing and testing equipment, and we provide continuous training so that our technical expertise keeps advancing. Integrity, efficiency, rigour and standardisation are our guiding principles. Committed to putting our customers' interests first, we provide every partner with quality products and the best possible pre-sales, in-sales and after-sales service.

🎯 Our Vision

To become a globally leading supplier of intelligent electronic products, driving industry advancement through innovation.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide customers with excellent intelligent electronic products and services, creating a smarter and more convenient lifestyle.
