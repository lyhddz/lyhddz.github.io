Outdoor lighting for villas

2026-10-28

MiBoxer European & American Villa Outdoor Lighting Solution（English Version）

1. Project Overview

This solution is designed for European & American single-family villas, high-end courtyards, and overseas residential projects.

The whole system adopts full MiBoxer smart low-voltage outdoor lighting system, fully compliant with European and American safety standards, with CE, RED, FCC, RoHS certifications and IP65–IP68 waterproof grade.

It is widely applicable to Europe, USA, Canada, Australia, Middle East, South Africa.

System Features

• Main warm white & CCT lighting for daily elegant villa appearance

• RGB colorful dynamic effects for Christmas, Halloween and parties

• Mobile APP timing, sunset auto-on, group control, scene mode

• 2.4G RF mesh signal relay, stable through walls and large gardens

• Full waterproof outdoor controllers & fixtures for long-term outdoor use

2. Lighting Zoning Design

1. Eaves outline lighting

2. Wall washer facade lighting

3. Garden tree spotlights

4. Pathway & landscape lights

5. Driveway & security floodlights

6. Optional: Swimming pool underwater lights
