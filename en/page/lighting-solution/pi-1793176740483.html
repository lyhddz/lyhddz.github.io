Luoyang International Hotel Outdoor Smart Lighting Solution（English Version）

1. Project Overview

Luoyang International Hotel is a five-star landmark business hotel located in Luolong District, Luoyang City. It features a unique sail-shaped main building, large glass curtain walls, European-style royal gardens, waterscape and fountain plaza.

This outdoor lighting upgrade adopts MiBoxer professional engineering smart low-voltage lighting system, focusing on business elegance, quiet luxury, zero light pollution and full-automatic intelligent control.
